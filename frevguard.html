One control-of-work system

Move beyond digital forms to an enforced safety process.

FrevGuard connects every permit decision to the task, location, equipment, hazards, controls, people and approvals behind it.

01

Site-specific by design

Configure permit types, plant hierarchy, risk matrices, checklists, roles and approval levels around each operating site.

02

Critical steps stay enforced

03

Permit lifecycle

Nine controlled stages from request to safe hand-back.

Each stage carries a clear owner, required evidence and a recorded decision before the permit advances.

  1. 01

    Request

    Define task, location, equipment and validity.

  2. 02

    Assess risk

    Identify hazards and assign mandatory controls.

  3. 03

    Isolate

    Map and verify energy isolation and LOTO.

  4. 04

    Gas test

    Record initial and scheduled atmospheric readings.

  5. 05

    Approve

    Route through configured operational and HSE roles.

  6. 06

    Issue

    Confirm site readiness, crew and toolbox talk.

  7. 07

    Execute

    Monitor live work, field checks and revalidation.

  8. 08

    Control change

    Suspend, extend or transfer with a reason trail.

  9. 09

    Close

    Confirm completion, de-isolation and hand-back.

Core capabilities

Visibility for the control room. Clarity for the field.

Give operations, HSE teams, issuers and contractors the controls they need without fragmenting the permit record.

PTW

Configurable permits

Hot work, confined space, electrical, height, excavation, line-break and site-specific permit templates.

JSA

Risk assessment

Task-based hazard libraries, configurable risk matrices and mandatory control validation.

LOTO

Isolation management

Equipment-linked isolation points, lock verification and controlled de-isolation sequences.

SLA

Approval governance

Role-based routing, delegation, escalation, rejection loops and recorded electronic approvals.

SIM

Concurrent-work awareness

Surface overlapping work by location, equipment and risk so conflicts can be assessed before issue.

AUD

Field assurance

Mobile inspections, gas-test schedules, evidence capture and a complete permit audit history.
