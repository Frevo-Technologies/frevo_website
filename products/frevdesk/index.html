FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Questions IT teams ask before a walkthrough.

Commercial details and current plan availability remain on the FrevDesk product site. These answers explain the operating model.

Explore all features ↗
01Are ITSM and ITAM separate products?

FrevDesk supports ITSM, ITAM or a combined suite. A team can start with the operating area it needs and use the product site to compare available plans and entitlements.

02Can emails automatically become tickets?

Yes. A company can connect a support mailbox so new messages create tickets and replies remain threaded against the correct service record, including permitted attachments.

03Can SLA rules vary by service or priority?

Response and resolution targets can be configured around the agreed service model, including relevant priorities, calendars, warnings and breach visibility.

04How are hardware and installed software discovered?

Windows endpoint discovery can collect device identity, hardware and installed software. Site-based discovery can identify supported devices, after which matching rules reconcile discoveries with governed records.

05Can we manage licences and consumables too?

Yes. The asset model supports software licences and quantity-based inventory alongside physical assets, including issue history, reorder exposure and valuation.

06How is each company’s data protected?

Tickets, users, assets, dashboards, reports and configuration are scoped to the logged-in company. Roles and permissions further control authorized actions.

07Can existing ticket and asset data be migrated?

Migration scope is agreed during discovery. Relevant users, assets, locations and reference data are mapped, validated and reconciled before controlled import.

08Where can I see pricing or start a trial?

Visit desk.frevo.co.in/pricing for current plans and commercial options, or use the product site registration path.
