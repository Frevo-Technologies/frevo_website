FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Questions permit and safety teams ask.

The exact workflow is confirmed against the site's approved permit procedure and operating responsibilities.

01Does FrevGuard approve permits automatically?

No. FrevGuard validates completeness and presents the required context. Approval or rejection remains an accountable human decision.

02Can permit types and checks vary by site?

Yes. Permit types, forms, mandatory evidence, roles and status paths are configured around the agreed operating process.

03Can isolation and gas-test evidence be attached?

Isolation references, verification context and time-stamped atmospheric results can remain associated with the relevant permit record.

04What happens when field conditions change?

The permit can move through governed states such as suspension, correction or revalidation according to the configured site process.

05Can contractors participate in the workflow?

Contractor roles and access are established during discovery so external work parties participate within controlled responsibilities.

06What remains in the audit history?

The record can preserve permit identity, evidence, responsible users, decisions, status changes, validity, hand-back and closure context.
